KE-SRCC collects and uses personal data for website registration and user management, the establishment and operation of the researcher database, the provision of personalised research information and recommendation services, service usage statistics analysis, and security and prevention of unauthorized use

B. Items of Personal Data Collected
General Members Mandatory items [Account info] User ID, Password, Full Name (English), Nationality, Date of Birth, Gender, E-mail Address [Affiliation info] Organization Type, Organization Name, Current Location, Research Area Optional items Full Name (Korean), Phone Number (including country code), Consent to receive marketing / informational e-mails Researcher Profile (Optional • For Researcher DB purposes; Public disclosure is optional) Affiliation Organization Type, Organization Name, Current Location, Country, Department, Current Position, Period of Employment Education Highest Degree, Institution of Graduation, Field of Study Research Research Domain, Keywords of Interest (Research Domain / Layer / Application)
